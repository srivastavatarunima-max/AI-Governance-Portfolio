# AI Governance Portfolio

A worked AI governance case study, **Case Study 01: AI-enabled recruitment screening**, written as a consulting-style set of deliverables. It follows one AI use case from registration through to an executive decision.

**Frameworks and regulations covered:** EU AI Act (including Annex III high-risk classification), UAE Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL, Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021), UAE Charter for the Development and Use of AI, ISO/IEC 42001 (AI management system) and the NIST AI Risk Management Framework.

> **Note:** All organisations, facts, ratings and recommendations in this portfolio are fictional and for demonstration. The client, GulfTalent Services PJSC, does not exist. Nothing here is legal advice or a certification assessment.

## The case

A fictional UAE-headquartered professional-services firm proposes to buy a third-party AI tool that parses job applications and ranks candidates for recruiters. The documents assess whether it should proceed, on what conditions, and what governance it needs.

## Documents

| # | Document | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI Governance Portfolio Case Study (use case registration) | Records the organisation, business need and proposed AI system before detailed assessment |
| 2 | AI Impact Assessment | Identifies who is affected and rates the impacts (overall rating: High) |
| 3 | AI Risk Register (PDF and Excel workbook) | Converts the impacts into 12 owned, scored risks with treatment actions and evidence requirements |
| 4 | Regulatory Applicability Assessment | Tests how the EU AI Act, UAE PDPL and UAE AI Charter apply, and what facts are still unknown |
| 5 | ISO/IEC 42001 AIMS Mapping | Maps the use case to an AI management system readiness view, with owners and evidence |
| 6 | Executive AI Governance Report | Gives leadership a recommendation (a conditional, controlled pilot), approval gates and a roadmap |
