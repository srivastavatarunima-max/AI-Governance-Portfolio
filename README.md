# AI Governance Portfolio

**Case Study 01: AI-enabled recruitment screening**

A fictional UAE employer, Gulf Professional Services PJSC, wants to buy a third-party AI tool that reads job applications and ranks candidates for its recruiters. The firm receives about 40,000 applications a year. This case study follows that one use case from registration through to an executive decision: should it go ahead, on what conditions, and what governance does it need?

**Outcome:** a controlled pilot only, once nine approval gates are evidenced. No production approval at current readiness.

**Frameworks covered:** ISO/IEC 42001 (AI management system, including Annex A controls), EU AI Act (Annex III high-risk classification, as amended by the Digital Omnibus), UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021), DIFC Data Protection Regulation 10, UAE Charter for the Development and Use of AI, NIST AI RMF and the OECD AI Principles.

> **Note:** Gulf Professional Services PJSC is fictional, and so are all facts, ratings and recommendations in this portfolio. Nothing here is legal advice, an audit or a certification assessment. Legal references reflect the position as at September 2026.

## How the documents connect

Each document builds on the one before it, and all of them share one use case ID (AI-UC-001).

1. **Registration** fixes the facts: what the tool does, who uses it, and what it must never do (reject candidates on its own).
2. **The impact assessment** asks who could be harmed. The answer is mainly applicants, through unfair ranking or never being reviewed at all.
3. **The risk register** scores those harms on a 5 × 5 likelihood × impact scale and assigns an owner and a deadline gate to each.
4. **The regulatory assessment** works out which laws apply. The answer depends on facts the firm has not yet confirmed, such as where the vendor sits and whether the firm is onshore or in a free zone.
5. **The ISO/IEC 42001 mapping** shows what the firm needs at organisation level (policy, Statement of Applicability, internal audit) to run this tool responsibly.
6. **The executive report** brings it together into one decision for leadership.
